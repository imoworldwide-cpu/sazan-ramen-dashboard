Key insights
❄️
Cold front = order more
When Austin drops below 65°F, expect 20–25% more orders than a typical warm day. Pre-order extra broth and noodles when you see a cold front in the forecast.
📅
Nov–Jan is your season
Winter months average $6,000+/day vs $4,300–4,700 in summer. Staff up and stock heavy from October onwards.
☀️
July–August bounce-back
Despite 99°F highs, orders tick up slightly vs June — Austin locals are heat-adapted and indoor dining becomes more appealing, not less, in peak summer.
🌧
Rain hurts weekends most
A rainy Saturday is your biggest risk day — it combines your highest-traffic day with an 18–22% traffic penalty. Watch Friday forecasts closely to adjust Saturday prep.
